About Tourism Grievance Management System (Tourism-GMS)

Government of Sikkim — Department of Tourism & Civil Aviation

Tourism-GMS is a centralized, digital dispute resolution platform built to protect the rights, safety, and hospitality experience of every visitor in Sikkim. Whether resolving overcharged prepaid taxi fares, hotel tariff disputes, unlicensed tour guide misconduct, or municipal sanitation issues, Tourism-GMS ensures every complaint receives immediate official oversight.

Once registered, tourists can lodge detailed grievances, attach inspection reports, track real-time resolution milestones, and communicate directly with assigned Officers.

Automated Load-Balancing

Complaints are instantly auto-assigned to departmental officers with the lowest workload.

Directorate Oversight

Strict SLA timelines with escalation controls supervised by Directorate Administrators.

Real-Time Audit Trail

Complete transparency from ticket submission to inspection report resolution.
